Bench Master Formula

Cutting flat or round wire to inside diameter alone results in a ring that is too small due to material stretching along the outer radius.

Inside Dia (ID) = (US Size × 0.032) + 0.466"
Mean Dia (MD) = ID + Wire Thickness
Cut Length = (MD × π) + Filing Buffer
